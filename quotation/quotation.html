Proposal for an online food-ordering website

Chef Subhajit’s kitchen, open for bulk orders.

Design and development of a Shopify website where Subhajit lists his dishes and sells them directly to catering businesses and corporate clients, with menus, ordering, payments and invoices set up for buyers who order in volume.

The brief

What Subhajit needs

Subhajit is a chef who wants to sell his food online to two kinds of buyers. Both order in volume and plan ahead, so the website is built around bulk ordering instead of single plates.

Buyer groupCatering businesses

  • Order by the tray or kilo, not by the plate
  • Minimum order quantities and better rates on volume
  • Book ahead for wedding, party and event dates
  • Reorder regular dishes in a few clicks

Buyer groupCorporate clients

  • Office lunches, meal boxes and event menus
  • Delivery on a chosen date and time slot
  • GST invoice with company name and GSTIN
  • Custom quotes for large or recurring orders
Both groups need Clear veg / non-veg marking Menus with real photos Secure online payment Quick WhatsApp contact

  5. Client content. The client provides the logo, dish names, photographs, descriptions, prices, FSSAI licence number, GSTIN (if registered) and policy details. Late content moves the launch date by the same number of days.

  6. Revisions. Two rounds of design revisions are included. Changes requested after design approval, or beyond two rounds, are charged separately.

  7. Payment. Work starts on receipt of the advance. Milestone payments are due within 7 days of each milestone. The website goes live only after the final payment is received.

  8. Taxes. The fee is exclusive of GST. Where applicable, GST will be charged at the prevailing rate (SAC 998314, IT design and development services).

  9. Cancellation. The advance is non-refundable once work has started. Payments for completed milestones are non-refundable if the project is cancelled.

  10. Ownership of work. On full payment, the website design, setup and content belong to the client. Jnanottam may show the website in a portfolio unless the client asks otherwise in writing.

  11. Support. 30 days of free support after launch covers bug fixes and small text or image changes. New features, redesigns and maintenance after this period are charged separately.

  12. Food business compliance. The client is responsible for food quality and safety, FSSAI licensing, pricing, delivery, GST on food sales and all other legal requirements of the food business. Jnanottam is responsible for the website only.

  13. Platform availability. Uptime, features and policies of Shopify, Razorpay and installed apps are controlled by those companies. Jnanottam will help resolve issues but is not liable for outages or changes on their side.

  14. Inactive projects. If the client does not respond for more than 30 days, the project may be put on hold, and restarting it may need a revised schedule.
